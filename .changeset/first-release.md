First release of Oak's shared agent skills and the `oak-skills` command-line tool. It includes the `oak-code-review`, `oak-pr-authoring`, `oak-security-review`, `oak-sos-draft` and `oak-accessibility-review` skills, grouped into the `base` and `frontend` profiles, and the `init`, `sync`, `status`, `check`, `promote` and `validate` commands.
